This policy applies to services operated by Hemerson Carlin. In this policy, "we" means Hemerson Carlin. This policy explains how we collect, use, store, and share personal data.

## Who is responsible for personal data

Hemerson Carlin operates services at hcarlin.com. Contact us at support@hcarlin.com with questions about personal data.

## Data the services collect

We may collect information a user provides. The information may include an email address, account details, messages, settings, and support requests.

We may collect technical information needed to operate and secure our services. The information may include IP address, device and browser information, and service logs.

## How the services use data

We use personal data to provide requested features, support users, secure our services, diagnose problems, and meet legal obligations.

## Connected accounts

Some services let a user connect a third-party account. We access a connected account only to provide a feature requested by the user.

Users can revoke access in the connected account's settings or request deletion by contacting us.

## Service providers

We share personal data with service providers only when a provider needs the data to operate a requested feature.

We do not sell personal data. We do not use personal data for advertising.

## Storage and security

We use reasonable safeguards to protect personal data. We retain personal data only for as long as needed to provide our services or meet legal obligations.

For services that store user data, we retain deleted data for 30 days before permanently deleting it.

## User choices

A user can request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal data by contacting us.

## Google services

Some of our services provide AI assistant features. Users can sign in with Google.

With a user's permission, we access Google account information, including Calendar data, to provide requested features. We store the account information and OAuth tokens needed for those features.

Google Workspace data is processed only through self-hosted LLM models on infrastructure that we operate. We do not send Google Workspace data any third-party AI provider for processing, training, or secondary use.

We share Google user data only with Google when acting on a user's behalf or where required by law. We do not sell Google user data or use it for advertising. Users can revoke access from their Google Account at any time.

We use and transfer information received from Google APIs in accordance with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
